Books and video series used across the visualization library

Video Series

Essence of Linear Algebra
3Blue1Brown (Grant Sanderson)
YouTube — 16 videos
Dot Product Cross Product Vectors Moment of a Force
Primary source for the linear algebra section. Builds geometric intuition for vectors, dot and cross products before any algebraic formalism.
Differential Equations
3Blue1Brown (Grant Sanderson)
YouTube — 8 videos
Heat Equation Fourier Series
Source for the heat equation lesson. The series covers ODEs, Fourier analysis, and the connection between eigenfunctions and boundary-value problems.

Textbooks — Engineering & Physics

Introduction to Space Dynamics
William T. Thomson
Dover Publications
Kinematics Circular Motion Euler Angles Particle Dynamics Orbital Mechanics Hohmann Transfer Gyrodynamics
Primary source for kinematics, particle dynamics, orbital mechanics, and gyrodynamics. Sections 4.7 (satellite orbits, Binet's substitution) and 4.9 (orbit from initial conditions, χ parameter, Fig 4.9-2) map directly to lessons.

Online Resources

Probability — Course Notes
Richard Weber
University of Cambridge, Statistical Laboratory · 2015
Probability Random Variables Distributions
Part IA course notes for first-year mathematicians at Cambridge. Self-contained lecture units covering classical probability, distributions, random walks, and generating functions. Includes examples sheets and a table of distributions.
