# Self-hosted resource mirrors

This folder exists to guard **link rot** for external resources — but only for
material we are actually allowed to redistribute. It is **not** a place for
copies of commercial textbooks.

## What may be mirrored here

A resource may be committed here only if it carries an explicit right to
redistribute, e.g.:

- an **open licence** (Creative Commons, MIT, public domain, etc.), or
- a statement from the author/publisher that it **may be freely distributed / mirrored**.

For each mirrored file, record in the table below: the file, the original URL,
and the licence/permission that allows the mirror. Keep the original attribution
inside or alongside the file.

## What may NOT be mirrored

- Commercial textbooks (e.g. Oppenheim & Willsky, *Signals and Systems*) — link to
  a purchase page, a library, or a legitimate free companion (e.g. MIT OCW) instead.
- "Free to download" PDFs that are **personal-use only**. Example: Gray & Davisson,
  *An Introduction to Statistical Signal Processing* — the author's page states the
  PDF "may be downloaded for use by individuals, but multiple copies may not be made
  without express permission from the authors and Cambridge University Press," so it
  is linked to the author's site, **not** mirrored here.
- Course notes / exam papers that are all-rights-reserved (most university teaching
  material) unless the licence explicitly permits redistribution.

When in doubt, link to the canonical source rather than mirror it.
